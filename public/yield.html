How the comparison works

The entry prices of one PT and one YT add up to 1 USDC here. Interest compounds at the annual yields you choose. Holding keeps all the interest. YT keeps 95% of it, valued in USDC with the accrued yield left in the asset. At expiry the right to future yield ends and earned interest is still claimable.

This example assumes full principal redemption and nonnegative yields. On Pendle, a fall in the underlying exchange rate can reduce PT redemption. Values are in USDC. Points, rewards, gas, swap costs and early-sale prices are outside this comparison.
