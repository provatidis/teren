How this lending model works

This is a hypothetical single-collateral loan based on Aave v3 collateral concepts. The rates, maximum LTV and liquidation threshold are editable teaching assumptions, not verified current Ethereum market parameters. No wallet or live lending data is used.

ETH represents supplied WETH, with wrapping costs excluded. USDC is fixed at $1. Borrowed USDC stays in the wallet without earning interest; it is not spent or reinvested. Supply interest increases ETH, and borrow interest increases USDC debt.

Choose simple interest or continuous compounding for both supply and debt. Simple interest grows only on the original amount. Continuous compounding also grows earlier interest, giving annual growth of exp(APR / 100) − 1. Rates stay fixed, and the year is 365 days. This is a mathematical comparison, not Aave’s reserve-index accounting or a rate forecast. Older scenarios and the existing lessons keep their simple-interest assumptions.

Health factor = collateral value × liquidation threshold / debt value. Below 1, the position is eligible for liquidation. With no debt, there is no loan liquidation boundary. Initial borrowing is limited by maximum LTV; later price and interest changes may move a loan above that ratio.

The separate liquidation illustration applies one event at your chosen price, using the requested repayment percentage and bonus. Available collateral caps the repayment. It separates debt repayment from the bonus cost and leaves unpaid debt visible if collateral runs out. It does not enforce Aave’s close-factor or dust rules or resolve protocol shortfalls.

The main loan result is an endpoint before liquidation. The model does not reconstruct the intervening path or execute a liquidation. It excludes oracle delays, market liquidity, caps, E-mode, additional assets and transaction costs. Inspect currently contains liquidity records; independently verified lending records are a later step.

How this borrowing-loop model works

Each round borrows the chosen fraction of the ETH value just supplied. The first uses your original ETH; later rounds use the preceding purchase. Swapping spends the borrowed USDC and applies the assumed percentage loss. Supplying then moves that ETH into collateral. No new outside capital is added.

All rounds happen at one starting price, before the selected time period. Supply interest adds ETH and borrow interest adds USDC debt. Choose fixed simple interest or continuous compounding; neither is exact Aave accounting. The single-loan lab offers the same two interest conventions; its older scenarios and foundation lessons retain simple interest.

The separate liquidation view uses already accrued collateral and debt, with no borrowed wallet cash left. It shows one assumed repayment plus bonus and keeps unpaid debt if collateral runs out. It does not change the headline, reference or unwind balances. The chart and headline values are pre-liquidation endpoints. The withdrawal-first unwind preview stops at or below health factor 1 and retains all debt. It runs at most 64 steps, showing unpaid debt if it cannot finish. It is not an execution plan or a protocol simulator. USDC = $1 is assumed. There is no wallet connection or live lending data.

How this experiment works

Build at the starting conversion rate, either in borrowing rounds or to an exact target. The target is a multiple of original wstETH holdings. Its flash-assisted purchase adds swap losses and a build flash fee to the financing needed; build gas is excluded. The maximum-LTV check does not establish available liquidity or executable transactions.

wstETH’s staking return changes the conversion rate; additional collateral yield changes the supplied token balance. The two yields are counted separately. APY is an annual growth factor. Borrow APR compounds continuously, with an optional rate change, over a 365-day year.

The model tracks ETH-denominated value. A dollar-price change alone cancels out of the collateral-to-debt ratio. A market discount can change sale proceeds without changing an oracle that tracks the conversion rate. A rate-only boundary reached earlier is flagged even if the later health factor recovers.

Both exit routes repay the debt and costs. Selling everything leaves WETH; selling only enough leaves unsold wstETH, which keeps its staking exposure and market risk. The first compares with selling the original holding under the same cost assumptions. The second compares with keeping the original tokens without an exit transaction.

The loop, thresholds and execution costs are hypothetical. Aave e-mode has category-specific rules; Morpho Blue markets have their own collateral, debt asset, oracle and liquidation threshold, and their collateral does not earn lending interest by default. Live rates, protocol caps, oracle limits, slashing events, withdrawal queues and real execution require separate evidence.
