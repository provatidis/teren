Ethereum mainnet · Historical contract reads and receipts

Trace the comparison

The entry purchases were simulated separately against the same pool state. Two actual transactions let us check the payout calculation against what the contracts sent to holders.

  • PT redemption ↗The first settlement locked the final interest index. The calculation reproduces the amount of the deposit wrapper (SY) the redeemer received.
  • YT interest claim ↗Using the holder’s prior balance and accrued interest reproduces the payments to the holder and the treasury to the smallest unit.
Entry, rate change and settlement blocks

Entry was fixed at the first block at or after 30 days before expiry. The 1,000 DAI budget was chosen before calculating returns. The separate 100 DAI comparison was chosen after the large YT order failed and before calculating outcomes.

The contract locked the final interest index 96 minutes and 47 seconds after scheduled expiry, when someone first settled. All three outcomes use that block. Settling in the first expiry block would have paid — on the 100 DAI YT purchase.

Trade size, fees and rewards

For each simulated purchase, a test account received DAI and permission to spend it. Pendle’s router used the pool as it stood at the entry block. The 1,000 DAI YT order exceeded this pool’s limit for a single direct swap. Other venues and later prices are outside this case.

The simulation includes trading fees and the effect of trade size. This market charged — on YT interest in 2024. The hypothetical yield lab uses 5%. The comparison keeps accrued interest in the yield-bearing asset until settlement, before gas. The recorded SY and YT contracts listed no reward tokens. No points or offchain incentives are valued.
